If you’re in Canada, read this first.

Pet food is not federally regulated in Canada. There is no Canadian agency that requires a manufacturer to announce a recall.

Most recalls that reach Canadian shelves are announced by the US FDA, because the same product is sold south of the border. That is why we watch the FDA feed for every user, not only Americans.

Silence does not mean safety. It means nobody was required to say anything.
